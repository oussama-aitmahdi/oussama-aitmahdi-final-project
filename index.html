a date that matters

november 26: protecting women and girls

26 November 2020 is significant in Morocco because the Economic, Social and Environmental Council (CESE) adopted an opinion declaring violence against women and girls a national emergency. This date highlights the importance of strengthening legal protection, particularly through Law 103.13 on combating violence against women. It reminds Morocco of its responsibility to prevent gender-based violence, protect survivors and guarantee women's and girls' fundamental rights. Ultimately, it reinforces the principle that protecting women and girls is a legal obligation and a fundamental human rights commitment.
