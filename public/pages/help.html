every situation follows the same six steps, so you always know where to look

street harassement

1. understand the situation

Street harassment is any unwanted behavior or attention directed at someone in a public place that makes them feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or intimidated. like unwanted comments, following, blocking the way, touching

2 . immediate actions
1 move toward a busy, place or a shop.
2 stay a clear, short "stop" and ask people nearby for help.
3 call someone you trust or the police if you feel unsafe.
3 . safety precautions

do not follow the person or argue if it could escalate. your safety comes first before proving a point

4 . evidence perservation

not date, time and place, photo or video if safe, names of witnesses, description of the person

5 . who to contact
police women's association lawyer
6 . possible legal options

Victims of street harassment can take legal action by filing a complaint with the police or relevant authorities. Depending on the law, they may have legal protections against harassment, threats, unwanted physical contact, and intimidation.

domestic violence

1. understand the situation

physical, verbal, psychological, sexual or economic violence by a spouse, partner or family member, often repeated and increasing.

2 . immediate actions
1 get to a safe place, or to someone you trust.
2 if injured, go to a hospital and ask for a medical certificate.
3 contact an association or the police to report and request protection.
3 . safety precautions

plan you exit quietly: keep documents, money and a bag ready. use a device the other person cannot check. leaving can be the most dangerous moment, so ask an association to help plan it.

4 . evidence perservation

medical certificate, photos of injuries and damage, thretening messages, dates and witnesses

5 . who to contact
police or gendarmerie women's association hospital
6 . possible legal options

Law 103-13 aims to protect women from all forms of violence by facilitating complaints, punishing perpetrators and providing victims with legal, medical and social protection.

see the guides

online harrasement

1. understand the situation

threts, blackmail, insults, impersination or sharing private images online

2 . immediate actions
1 do not reply or pay, take screenshots first.
2 report and block the amount on the platform.
3 secure your accounts: change passwords, turn on two step login.
3 . safety precautions

never send money or more images to a blackmailer. do not delete anything before saving proof.

4 . evidence perservation

screenshots with date and time, profile links and usernames, original messages kept, payement requests

5 . who to contact
police (cybercrime unit) psychological support lawyer
6 . possible legal options

Moroccan law protects people and organizations against illegal access, hacking, data theft or manipulation, and attacks on computer systems. People responsible may face imprisonment and financial penalties..
